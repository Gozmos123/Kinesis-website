WHO WE ARE

A student-driven org growing the next generation of racket-sport officials

Kinesis Organization is a non-profit, educational organization based at City College of Angeles in Angeles City, Philippines. We exist to make officiating education accessible — starting with the fastest-growing racket sports around: pickleball and paddle sports.

Through structured seminars and real court practice, we help students and community members understand the rules, mechanics, and judgment calls that keep matches fair — turning enthusiastic players into confident, capable officials.

WHAT WE OFFER

Programs built for the court

From first-time volunteers to future certified officials, our programs meet you where you are.

Officiating Seminars & Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering rules, court mechanics, and match management for pickleball and paddle sports — like our flagship "Smash and Paddle" workshop.

Certification Prep

A guided pathway that builds the rules knowledge, positioning, and confidence needed to move toward official certification.

Community Clinics

Open sessions for players and spectators to learn the game from the official's side of the net — building respect for the role and the rules.

Campus Partnerships

Collaborating with schools and sports organizations to bring officiating education to more athletes across Angeles City and beyond.
