Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and getting involved with Kinesis.

Do I need experience to join Kinesis? +

No prior officiating experience is required. Our seminars and workshops are built to take you from complete beginner to confident on the court.

Is membership free? +

Yes — creating a Kinesis account is completely free. It gives you a profile, access to member resources, and priority registration for events.

Who can join? +

Kinesis is open to students, players, and community members interested in learning how to officiate pickleball and paddle sports — you don't need to be enrolled at City College of Angeles to participate in most events.

How do I register for an event? +

Sign up for a free account, then head to your Dashboard — upcoming events will show a Register button right there.

Where are events held? +

Most events take place at the City College of Angeles (CCA) Covered Court in Angeles City, Philippines. Exact venues are listed on each event's details.

How can I get involved beyond attending events? +

Reach out through our Contact page — we're always looking for volunteers, campus partners, and community collaborators.
