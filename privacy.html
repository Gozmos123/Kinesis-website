Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Kinesis Organization ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and password (securely handled by our authentication provider, Firebase).
  • Event registration: If you register for an event, we store your name, email, and the event you registered for.
  • Contact form submissions: If you message us through the Contact page, we receive whatever information you choose to include.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process event registrations and send related updates
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To understand overall site usage through basic analytics

How We Store Your Information

Account and registration data is stored using Google Firebase, a third-party service with its own security practices. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers.

Your Choices

You can update your profile information at any time while logged in. To request deletion of your account or data, contact us using the details below.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use it. These tools do not collect personally identifiable information beyond what you voluntarily provide.
